Checksum · All releases

Easy to install

Start from a USB stick, connect to Wi-Fi and answer a few questions. Nothing changes until you say yes.

Your hardware, set up

Wi-Fi, graphics and processor drivers are found and installed for you, including the fixes Intel Macs need.

Mistakes can be undone

Snapshots are taken before risky updates, and you can start your computer from one right from the boot menu.

Updates take care of themselves

Once a week kirkus updates the system, your apps and itself, when you're plugged in and it won't get in your way.

Install in three steps

  1. Make a USB stick

    Download kirkus above, then write it to a USB stick of 4 GB or more with balenaEtcher. Everything on the stick is erased.

  2. Start from it

    On a PC, press its boot menu key at power-on (often F12, F11 or Esc). On a Mac, hold Option ⌥ and choose EFI Boot.

  3. Click Install kirkus

    Pick your keyboard and disk, choose a username and password, and in 10 to 30 minutes you're done.

Keeping Windows or macOS alongside it? Read the full guide first.

What you need

  • A 64-bit Intel or AMD PC, or an Intel Mac. Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) aren't supported.
  • 15 GB of free disk space (30 GB is comfortable) and 4 GB of memory.
  • A USB stick of 4 GB or more.
  • An internet connection while installing.
